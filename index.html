Patient Readmission Prediction

A machine learning classifier that flags patients likely to be readmitted to hospital, so care teams can plan follow-up earlier.

Problem
Hospital readmissions are costly and often preventable, but hard to spot from raw patient records by hand.
Approach
Cleaned and preprocessed the data, explored it visually, encoded categorical features, scaled numeric ones, and trained a Random Forest Classifier.
Results
Evaluated with accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score, with attention to recall because missed readmissions matter most.
Tech
PythonPandasNumPyMatplotlibScikit-learn
